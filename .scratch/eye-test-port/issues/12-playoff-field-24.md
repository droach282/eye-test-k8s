# 12: 24-team playoff field

**What to build:** The 24-team format from the 2027 season: Group of Six leader bid, no Notre Dame rule, seeds 1-8 with byes, and the second-round pairings.

**Blocked by:** 11

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] Format selected by season year alone
- [ ] Group of Six leader is the single highest-ranked team across all six conferences
- [ ] Golden fixtures match their hand-derived expected results
- [ ] Group of Six leader is the highest-ranked team from the American, Conference USA, MAC, Mountain West, Pac-12 or Sun Belt; an independent never takes the bid
- [ ] Notre Dame has no rule of its own
- [ ] Opening round: seeds 9 through 16 meet seeds 24 down to 17; seed 1 meets the winner of 16v17, down to seed 8 against the winner of 9v24
- [ ] The field can be derived from the ranking of any snapshot, at any week, not only a season's last
